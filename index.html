IEEPA Tariff Refunds — 2025–2026 Entries

The IEEPA tariffs were ruled unlawful. We help you get them back.

If you're an importer of record who paid IEEPA tariffs, the U.S. government likely owes you a refund, plus accrued interest. Let our team review your refund exposure now.

Request a Free Entry Evaluation
⚠

If you have not filed at the CIT and your entries are finally liquidated, the government's appeal of the CIT's refund order — now pending at the Federal Circuit — could leave your refund waiting indefinitely, or excluded from CAPE Phase 3 altogether. Since every entry runs on its own clock, your deadlines for administrative relief may be sooner than you think. A protective filing preserves your refund rights. We explain your options in plain language — no obligation to proceed. Everything you share is kept confidential.
